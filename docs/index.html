About this map

Limits first: this is a screening-grade topological dataset. Positions, voltages and ratings carry per-value provenance (sourced:, inferred: or unknown) shown verbatim in the popups. Do not use it as an asset register or for operational decisions.

Planned developments are compiled from public network development plans (ENTSO-E TYNDP 2026 project material and national/TSO plans — NESO "Beyond 2030", Terna PdS 2025, RTE's project catalogue, MITECO's 2024 planning modification, and the plan cited on each feature). Service years are promoter-stated, shown verbatim — not forecasts, not delivery promises. Except where marked as sourced plan geometry, project lines are straight schematics between matched public endpoint locations — not route alignments.

Scenario circles share national TYNDP 2026 figures (© ENTSO-E TYNDP 2026 Scenarios, CC BY 4.0; NT+ = National Trends+, LEV/HEV = Low/High Economy Variant) out to OSM populated places (demand, by population) and OSM power plants (generation, by technology and rating). They are inferred: visualization weights for screening context — not forecasts, not dispatch, and this map computes no power flows. The economy variants alter demand; their installed-capacity mix is nearly identical, so generation circles barely move between NT+/LEV/HEV — that is the scenarios, not a bug.

Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Method after Xiong et al. (2025), with thanks to PyPSA-Eur and pandapower. Basemap by OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles.
