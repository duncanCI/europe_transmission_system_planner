About this map

Limits first: this is a screening-grade topological dataset. Positions, voltages and ratings carry per-value provenance (sourced:, inferred: or unknown) shown verbatim in the popups. Do not use it as an asset register or for operational decisions.

Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Method after Xiong et al. (2025), with thanks to PyPSA-Eur and pandapower. Basemap by OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles.
