About this map

Limits first: this is a screening-grade topological dataset. Per-value provenance travels only with the values that carry a *_source column — line labels, circuit counts, frequency, DC link ratings and transformer parameters — and those source values are shown verbatim in the popups. Positions, voltages and AC line ratings have no provenance column. Every AC line rating in the dataset is a typed value inferred from voltage and circuit count (a standard conductor type applied by rule), not a measured or operator-published rating. Do not use it as an asset register, as investment evidence, or for operational decisions.

Planned developments are compiled from public national and TSO network development plans (NESO "Beyond 2030", Terna PdS 2025, RTE's project catalogue, MITECO's 2024 planning modification, and the plan cited on each feature). Service years are promoter-stated, shown verbatim — not forecasts, not delivery promises. Except where marked as sourced plan geometry, project lines are straight schematics between matched public endpoint locations — not route alignments.

ENTSO-E TYNDP 2026 projects are the pan-European portfolio, shown alongside the national plans and published here with ENTSO-E's permission (the portfolio is ENTSO-E's, not open data — see LICENCE_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md). Dashed cyan lines are transmission projects and dots are storage; translucent violet-blue fills are study corridors and areas — broad zones under study, not routes and not lines. Commissioning years are ENTSO-E's own and are gated by the year slider; they are not forecasts or delivery promises.

Scenario circles share national TYNDP 2026 figures (© ENTSO-E TYNDP 2026 Scenarios, CC BY 4.0; NT+ = National Trends+, LEV/HEV = Low/High Economy Variant) out to OSM populated places (demand, by population) and OSM power plants (generation, by technology and rating). They are inferred: visualization weights for screening context — not forecasts, not dispatch, and this map computes no power flows. The economy variants alter demand; their installed-capacity mix is nearly identical, so generation circles barely move between NT+/LEV/HEV — that is the scenarios, not a bug.

What the circles leave out. The circles draw only places of ≥5,000 population and plants of ≥10 MW OSM weight, so they carry part of each national total — 49–96% of a country's demand, and as little as 0.26% of a given country-and-technology capacity where the fleet is mostly sub-10 MW sites (Greek solar). Shares are deliberately not renormalised onto the circles that survive the cutoff: that would keep the national sum right by labelling a 10 MW site with gigawatts. Where a coverage figure can be computed, the popup states it; a few country-and-technology combinations have no TYNDP total to be a fraction of, and say so rather than guess. Read the circles as where capacity and demand sit, never as a national total. Plant weights keep their own provenance too: where OSM publishes no rating the popup says so and shows the assumed default (the median of sourced OSM ratings for that technology) as a separate, clearly labelled row — never as an OSM figure. Battery storage is the separate Flexibility layer and is excluded from the generation-capacity readout.
